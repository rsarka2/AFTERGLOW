WHAT THE DRONE IS LISTENING FORFIND MY · FIND HUB · SAMSUNG · LoRa
How it finds someone: hears a phone or tag on the trail sweep → flies toward the strongest signal and pins it down → drops to 25 m and checks with thermal + mmWave → proposes the pin to the ranger, who confirms or clears it. Then it moves on, and finally searches off-trail where lost people usually end up.

Research prototype. Trail traffic is a proxy model from OSM features (plus known high-use routes in case presets) — not measured counts. Elevation is a ~10–30 m DEM without trees, towers or wires. RF, thermal and mmWave behaviour are simplified models with assumed parameters. Case-study positions are reconstructions: news reports do not give exact locations. Drone flight in National Parks needs NPS authorization; real operations need FAA compliance and trained SAR oversight. A beacon hit is not proof a person is present.
