SHEET 2Layout inside the pod

Where each part sits, to the CAD's millimetres. The pod is 140 × 100 × 55 mm; the sensor face points forward and down. Hover a name to find it.

  1. 1Raspberry Pi 5 + Active Cooler on the sled, USB ports to the rear
  2. 2Up-angled USB adapters rear of the Pi, to LoRa and radar
  3. 3OMNI ESP32 left bay
  4. 4WIFI ESP32 right bay
  5. 5LoRa board right bay, ahead of WIFI
  6. 6Board antennas the stick-on antenna from each XIAO's box, one per side wall
  7. 7Thermal camera sensor face, right, in the 12 mm hole
  8. 8Radar sensor face, left, behind a 1 mm window
  9. 9PWR slot + USB-C plug left wall; the 5 V pigtail from the drone's converter plugs into the Pi
  10. 10LORA port (SMA) rear wall
  11. 11Lid thumbscrew bosses four corners
  12. 12Sled rests on four ledges at 10 mm
  13. 13Lid drops in on its lip
  14. 14Top plate four M3 screws; straps or a mount hold it to the drone
  15. 15IMU left bay, between OMNI and the PWR slot
  16. 16AI HAT+ stacked on the Pi (dashed outline); runs person detection
  17. 17Camera Module 3 sensor face, centre, in a 10 mm hole you drill
  18. 18On the drone, not drawn flight battery, 5 V converter, GPS on the top plate; their leads enter by the PWR slot and a side vent
